How I migrate a live estate

The same four steps on every programme since 2018, whatever the vendor. My training is in engineering rather than IT, in disciplines where the process and the documentation are the deliverable, and this is what that turned into.

Step 1

Build alongside

The new platform goes in live, next to the old one, on temporary addressing. Nothing is decommissioned to make room for it.

Step 2

Prove before the window

Every interface tested while there is still time to fix it. Faulty optics, duplex mismatches and bad patching surface in daylight, not at 2am.

Step 3

Cut over small

The change itself is the smallest possible act: move the addressing, enable an interface, move an extension into a different partition.

Step 4

Verify, then hold

A documented rollback at every step, with the test that proves it worked. The old kit stays powered off for a while before it goes.

Migrate one first. Watch it. Write down what surprised you. Then repeat the procedure you have just proved for the remaining ninety-nine.
